TrueGram

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

TrueGram is a macro tracker. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your food log, goals, weight and notes are stored on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch, if you use it). This page lists every case where something leaves your phone, and why.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you log: foods, meals, amounts, water, creatine, notes, goals and completed days.
  • Your bodyweight, height, age and activity level.
  • Photos you save to log later.
  • Anything TrueGram reads from or writes to Apple Health.

The widgets and the Apple Watch app get their numbers from your phone directly, not from a server.

What leaves your phone

Food lookups

When you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode or search words are sent to the USDA FoodData Central database and, for barcodes it doesn't have, to Open Food Facts. Nothing about you is sent with them.

Photo and voice estimates, and label reading

Only when you tap Estimate, or when a Nutrition Facts label can't be read on the phone, the photo and any text you typed or said are sent to Anthropic, which makes the Claude AI model. Claude returns the estimate or the label's numbers. Nothing else from your log goes with it. Anthropic handles this data under its commercial terms and privacy policy, which say API data is not used to train its models.

Shared label fixes

When you fix a packaged food to match its label, TrueGram can share that fix so the next person who scans the barcode gets the right numbers. What is sent: the barcode, the food's name, serving sizes and nutrition, and a random ID made by the app on your phone, so two fixes from the same phone count once. Nothing about you is sent. The fixes are stored on TrueGram's server. Turn this off any time in Settings with Share my label fixes.

TrueGram's server

Food lookups and estimates may go through TrueGram's own server on the way to USDA and Anthropic, so the app doesn't need to hold those services' keys. The server passes the request along and does not keep it. It sees your IP address, like any website, and uses it only to stop too many requests at once. It is not stored.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, TrueGram writes your food, water and weigh-ins to Health, and reads weigh-ins, workouts, steps and active calories. Health data is used only to show your numbers and pick training-day goals inside the app. It is never sent to TrueGram's server, Anthropic or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never sold. You can change what TrueGram can read and write any time in the Health app.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes and labels and to photograph meals. Photos you pick from your library are read only when you choose them. A photo is sent anywhere only as described under Photo and voice estimates.

Notifications

Meal and weigh-in reminders are scheduled on your phone. No push server is involved.

Children

TrueGram is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

  • Export your whole log any time with Export my log (CSV) in Settings.
  • Delete all my data, in the Me tab, erases your log, foods and goals from your phone. Deleting the app does the same.
  • To remove label fixes you shared, email the address below with the barcode. Since they carry no name or account, we can't find them any other way.
