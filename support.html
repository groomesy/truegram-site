The app has a full Help and FAQ in Settings, with search.

Common questions

A barcode shows the wrong numbers.

Tap the food you logged, then Scan the label to fix it. The fixed food is marked verified and wins every future scan of that barcode.

Why did it ask me to scan the label?

TrueGram checks that protein, carbs and fat add up to the listed calories. When a database entry doesn't, it asks for the label instead of logging numbers that are probably wrong.

How do I log a meal at a restaurant?

In Add food, tap Restaurants for Chipotle, Chick-fil-A and Jersey Mike's. Anywhere else, tap Photo or Voice and list each part of the meal. Each part gets its own amount and macros.

Can I take a photo now and log it later?

Yes. Take the photo, then tap Save for later. It waits in that meal as Photo to log.

How do I switch between eaten and left?

Tap Today's Facts.

What do the bar colors mean?

Green is on your goal, amber is close, red is off by more. Bars stay black while you're still eating under your goal, and color in once the day is marked complete.

Does it work offline?

Logging, your foods, saved meals, common foods and goals all work offline. Searching USDA and photo estimates need a connection.

How do I get my data out?

In Settings, tap Export my log (CSV).

Is my data private?

There is no account, and your log stays on your phone. See the privacy policy for the few things that leave it.
